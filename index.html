400nm – 700nm · about the chapter

A student community built around light

IEEE Photonics Society is the world's leading professional organization for laser, optical, and photonic technologies. The SSN Student Chapter brings that global community to campus — connecting students with research, industry, and each other.

IEEE Photonics Society advances knowledge and application of optics and photonics for the benefit of humanity, spanning lasers, fiber optics, displays, sensors, and quantum photonics.

The SSN Student Chapter was formed to give students at SSN College of Engineering direct access to this world — through hands-on workshops, expert talks, competitions, and collaborative projects that go beyond the classroom syllabus.

We exist to make photonics tangible: not just equations, but the lived experience of splicing a fiber, aligning a laser, or watching a signal travel as pure light.
